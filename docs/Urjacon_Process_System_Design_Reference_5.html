Everything under "process" describes how work is actually done. The data model, access matrix, reports and suggested statuses are proposals derived from that process and are marked as such.

4. Stage by stage

Each stage with its owner, start and end condition, steps, the data to capture, and the rules and holds that apply. Hover a rule or hold code to read it.

ALead and documents

Turn a phone number or visit into a complete document packet, or close the lead.

Owner
Marketing: Bhojraj Nayak and 15 field staff. Calling: 7 executives under Ankita Ahuja. Also agents (commission) and office connections.
Starts when
A number from CSEB or other data, a field visit, an agent or an office connection.
Ends when
Complete packet handed to Ankita Ahuja, or the lead is marked lost.
Turnaround
No fixed time; the lead stays open until documents or a final no.

Steps

  • 1
    Lead sourced and added
  • 2
    Follow-up and quotation. Each contact ends in one of three outcomes: customer gives a call-back time, is not interested, or gives documents (sometimes a visit is needed to collect them)
  • 3
    Document packet collected

Data to capture

Source, agent, name, mobile, village / address, consumer no. if known, monthly bill, kW wanted, product code, hot / warm / cold, next call date and time, outcome, lost reason, quotation reference, payment mode, commission (agent case).

BVerification and login

Check the documents, fix name or ownership problems, and log the consumer in on the national portal.

Owner
Ankita Ahuja receives. Reshmi (Maahi) verifies. Tripti / Rashmi log in. Soniya with Ankita Sikka prepares corrections; Satya / Khemdas (Alok for Sarangarh) file them at CSEB. Khusboo schedules the survey; service team surveys.
Starts when
Complete packet received.
Ends when
Corrections done and corrected bill received, logged in with an Application No., survey report ready.
Turnaround
Correction: about 2 working days in CG, about 15 days in Odisha. Failed login retried next day.

Steps

  • 4
    Documents received and forwarded to Reshmi
  • 5
    Documents verified and saved in the database; system marked Tata or non-Tata; consumer called to confirm and told about the OTP
  • 6
    If needed: owner change, name correction or new PAN. The case waits here until the corrected bill comes; only then login and loan go ahead
  • 7
    Login on the national portal with the consumer's OTP
  • 8
    Site survey with a detailed site form, started in parallel with login

Data to capture

Verification result, system type (Tata / non-Tata), correction type and reason, filing date, corrected bill date, login date and by whom, Application No., login hold reason and retry date, survey date, surveyor, site form, smart meter present or not, material needed.

GClosure

Recover every rupee due, pass on the subsidy, process warranty by panel make, and close the case.

Owner
Banking team (30%), Soniya (subsidy), accounts (balance recovery, cheque deposit, commission), service team (warranty).
Starts when
Inspection approved on the national portal.
Ends when
Balance cleared, warranty done, 30% received (loan) and subsidy disbursed.
Turnaround
Not fixed.

Steps

  • 27
    Soniya redeems the subsidy; the state subsidy follows the central one on its own. Cheque case: cheques deposited once the subsidy reaches the consumer
  • 28
    Banking team submits invoice, work completion and plant geotag to the bank
  • 30
    Bank releases the last 30%; agent receives the balance commission
  • 29
    Accounts recover any balance from the consumer (cash: last 10%; any down payment or other amount due)
  • —
    Balance pending: no warranty, no document handover, plant kept off where possible
  • 31
    Warranty by panel make. Tata: Tata's warranty process. Non-Tata: panel warranty through the DCR, inverter warranty registered online. Customer receives warranty documents, invoice and the consumer-vendor agreement

Data to capture

Subsidy redeem date, central and state disbursal dates, cheque deposit and clearing (cheque case), 30% file date, 30% amount and date, balance due and recovered, warranty type (Tata / non-Tata), Tata warranty reference, inverter online registration, warranty date, handover kit given, commission paid.

7. Business rules

Conditions the system must enforce or check. Codes are referenced from the stage sections.

CodeRule
BR-01A lead stays open until the customer gives documents or a final no. Every open lead has a next call date and a hot / warm / cold tag.
BR-02Office work starts only when the handover packet is complete: PAN, electricity bill, Aadhaar, passbook, terrace geotag, phone, email, product code, payment mode, kW, and the commission amount in agent cases.
BR-03Owner change is needed when the bill owner is above 65 or the consumer asks for it. A name spelling mismatch must be corrected. A PAN card must exist.
BR-04Correction or owner change comes first. Loan cases are logged in, and the loan is started, only after the corrected bill is received.
BR-05The consumer is told about the OTP before login. If the call does not connect or the OTP is not shared, login is retried the next day.
BR-06The site survey is scheduled as soon as login starts, in parallel.
BR-07The consumer-vendor agreement is uploaded on the portal before the loan is processed. Stamp: ₹50 in Chhattisgarh, ₹10 in Odisha.
BR-08Bank files are assigned by area: Sarangarh to Alok, Padampur to Kishan, Bargarh / Sohela to Pankaj, Jharband to Mahesh, Saraipali to Akash.
BR-09The bank file needs income proof above 3 kW, and ghar ka patta in Odisha.
BR-10A rejected loan goes back to Reshmi. Curable: clear the reason and redo JanSamarth. Not curable: Reshmi and Ankita Ahuja decide to convert to cash, retry, or drop.
BR-11Loan case dispatch: accounts sign the cash receipt confirming 70% received and no down payment pending.
BR-12Cash case dispatch: accounts sign the cash receipt for 90%. Below 90%, only with the MDs' approval named on the receipt. The DCR is made only after 90% is received, so the plant cannot be commissioned before that.
BR-13Subsidy-cheque case (Odisha) dispatch: same as cash. Accounts sign the receipt confirming the full balance (price minus subsidy), both subsidy cheques (central, state) and the signed agreement.
BR-14Material is dispatched as per the survey report.
BR-15Odisha: the TPWODL reference is created at dispatch so the meter charge can be paid before installation finishes.
BR-16Installation is submitted on the national portal only after all panel serial photos, the inverter serial photo, the plant geotag, the bill and the DCR are in.
BR-17Chhattisgarh: if there is no smart meter, the surveyor flags it so a smart meter is fitted through Genus while the rest of the work goes on. If a smart meter exists, a generation meter is issued and fitted by the service team.
BR-18The plant can be switched on before portal approval only when the meter is bidirectional.
BR-19Portal inspection is approved only after the plant is configured and the inverter login is shared.
BR-20The subsidy is redeemed only after portal inspection approval. The state subsidy follows the central subsidy automatically.
BR-21The final 30% bank file (invoice, work completion, plant geotag) is submitted after commissioning.
BR-22Cash case: the last 10% is collected before warranty.
BR-23Agent commission. Loan: 70% once the bank's 70% and any down payment are in, balance when the 30% is disbursed. Subsidy cheque: all of it after the subsidy comes. Cash cases have no agents.
BR-24A case closes when the consumer has no balance pending, the bank's 30% (loan) or cheques (cheque case) are in, the subsidy is disbursed and warranty is done.
BR-25Any process issue or customer complaint goes to Khusboo Panda, who routes it to the responsible person.
BR-26No dispatch in any payment mode without a cash receipt from accounts, signed by the accountant. If money has not come in, the receipt states whose approval the dispatch is on.
BR-27Dispatch without cash or down payment needs the MDs' approval.
BR-28Before warranty, accounts recover any balance from the consumer. While any amount is pending: no warranty, no document handover, and the plant is not switched on where that is possible.
BR-29Reshmi (Maahi) marks every case Tata or non-Tata at verification. Warranty depends on this: Tata systems follow Tata's warranty process. Non-Tata systems: panel warranty through the DCR, inverter warranty registered online.

15. Worked examples

Chhattisgarh loan, agent case, name correction

  1. An agent brings a customer. The packet reaches Ankita Ahuja with the commission amount noted.
  2. Reshmi finds a spelling mismatch on the bill. Soniya prepares the correction, Khemdas files it at the CSEB office, and the corrected bill comes in about 2 working days.
  3. Tripti logs the consumer in with the OTP. The survey starts the same day and notes that the house has no smart meter.
  4. Soniya uploads the agreement (₹50 stamp). Reshmi runs JanSamarth and hands the bank file to Alok, who gives it to the area person.
  5. The bank visits the house (PHS) and releases 70%. The loan is short of the price, so Ankita Ahuja collects the down payment.
  6. Chetan signs the cash receipt and the agent becomes due 70% of the commission. Sameer dispatches per the survey report.
  7. After installation the installer sends serial photos and the geotag. Chetan makes the bill and DCR; Soniya submits on the portal.
  8. Satya files the inspection at CSEB and gets a smart meter fitted through Genus, then takes the JE to site. The service team configures the plant.
  9. After portal approval: 30% file to the bank and subsidy redeemed. Accounts confirm nothing is due from the consumer. It is a Tata system, so warranty follows Tata's process. The 30% arrives, the agent gets the balance, and the case closes.

Odisha subsidy-cheque case

  1. System price ₹1,80,000, subsidy ₹1,38,000. The consumer pays ₹42,000, gives two cheques (central and state subsidy) and signs the agreement.
  2. No loan steps. Chetan checks the balance, cheques and agreement and signs the cash receipt; only then is material dispatched.
  3. At dispatch the TPWODL reference is created and the meter charge is paid.
  4. Installation, bill, DCR and portal submission as usual. DCR and work completion go up on the TPWODL portal; the TPWODL JE checks the plant and TPWODL fits the meter.
  5. Service team configures the plant; portal inspection is approved. Soniya redeems the subsidy.
  6. When the subsidy reaches the consumer, the two cheques are deposited. If an agent brought the case, the full commission is paid now. It is a non-Tata system: panel warranty through the DCR, inverter warranty online. Then closed.

Chhattisgarh cash case below 90%

  1. The customer has paid less than 90%. MDs approve dispatch; the accountant writes their approval on the cash receipt, and material goes out.
  2. The plant is installed, but Chetan does not make the DCR, so the installation cannot be submitted and the plant cannot be commissioned.
  3. Once 90% is in, the DCR is made and the case continues: portal submission, inspection, meter, JE, configuration, approval.
  4. Before warranty, accounts check the balance. Until the last 10% is paid there is no warranty and no document handover. No agent is involved.

16. Glossary

PMSG / PM Surya Ghar
Central rooftop solar scheme; subsidy for residential plants.
National portal
The PM Surya Ghar vendor portal: login, agreement, installation, inspection, subsidy.
JanSamarth
Government loan platform used for the solar loan; ends in the bank's digital approval.
CSEB / CSPDCL
Chhattisgarh discom. Work is done at its offices on paper.
TPWODL
TP Western Odisha Distribution Ltd, the Odisha discom; work is done on its customer portal.
BP no. / SC no.
Consumer number on the electricity bill (BP in CG, SC in Odisha).
Application No.
National portal ID, e.g. NP-CGSPD… (CG) or NP-ODTWO… (Odisha).
Owner change
Changing the name on the electricity connection to another person.
Consumer-vendor agreement
Agreement between consumer and Urjacon uploaded on the portal; stamp ₹50 CG, ₹10 OD.
PHS
The bank's home visit to the consumer during loan checks.
Down payment
Part of the price the consumer pays when the loan is less than the price.
DCR
Domestic content (made-in-India panel) certificate made by accounts; needed to submit installation.
Work completion
Completion document for the installed plant; part of inspection and the 30% file.
PPA
Power purchase agreement; part of the CG inspection file.
Geotag
Location-stamped photo (terrace before, plant after).
Generation meter / smart meter
Meters needed for net metering; the meter must be bidirectional before the plant runs.
Development charges
TPWODL charge for the meter, paid at dispatch.
Service released
Final TPWODL status: connection released.
Reverification / Duplicate Sr. No.
National portal flags: recheck needed / a serial number is already used.
Subsidy redeem
Claiming the subsidy on the portal after inspection approval.
Subsidy-cheque case
Odisha payment mode: consumer pays price minus subsidy and gives two subsidy cheques.
Product code
Code for the system the customer is buying.
Cash receipt
Receipt from accounts, signed by the accountant, needed for every dispatch; names the approver if money is short.
Tata / non-Tata system
System type, marked by Reshmi (Maahi) at verification; decides the warranty process.
Hot / warm / cold
Lead temperature that sets call priority.
